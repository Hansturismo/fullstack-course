About Eataly

Eataly was founded in 1996 by husband and wife, Marco and Maria, coming straigth from Puglia, Italy.
Eataly is ran today by Marco and Maria, and their children and family.
Our passion for authentic flavors and traditional recipes drives everything we do, which means where it makes sense, we source some ingredients directly from Italy.
